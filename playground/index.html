The radius says how much a corner is rounded: it is the radius of the circle drawn in the corner. On a rectangle the curve starts that many pixels before the corner; on a polygon it starts sooner at sharp corners, later at wide ones. The requested radius is the number you type; it is kept. The effective radius is the one drawn: smaller when the shape is too small for the request. A polygon has 3 to 12 equal sides and a flat base; it fills its width × height box without being stretched. The grey rectangle is that box. The anchor says where the polygon sits in the room the box leaves: one of nine places, from top left to bottom right; the center by default. Sizes are screen pixels: a value above 100 000 becomes 100 000.
